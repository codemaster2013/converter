Questions

Which files can it convert?PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), images (JPG, PNG, WebP and more), HTML and text files. Excel files are not supported. Old .doc and .ppt files must be saved as .docx or .pptx first.
Will my Word or PowerPoint file look exactly the same?Text, pictures, tables and shapes are kept. Very complex designs, charts and special fonts can look different.
Can I edit a PDF in Word?Yes. PDF to Word keeps the text editable. Scanned PDFs are kept as pictures; use Smart OCR to read their text.
Are my files uploaded?No. Everything is converted on your device.

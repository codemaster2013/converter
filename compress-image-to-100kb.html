How it works

  1. Upload one or more images.
  2. Pick a size limit (20, 50, 100, 200, 500 KB or your own).
  3. Download the compressed images, or all of them as a ZIP.

Why use it here

  • Free, with no sign-up and no watermark.
  • Your files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.
  • Install it as an app and use it offline after the first visit.

Questions

What if the image cannot get that small?The tool lowers the quality first and then the pixel size. If it still cannot fit, it shows the smallest result and tells you.
Will it look blurry?Very small limits reduce detail. Larger limits keep more quality.
Are images uploaded?No. Compression happens on your device.

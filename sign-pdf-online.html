Sign a PDF

Draw your signature with a finger or mouse, drag it to the right spot on the page, and download the signed PDF. This adds a picture of your signature; it is not a certified digital signature.

Open the tool

How it works

  1. Upload the PDF.
  2. Draw your signature and tap Use this signature.
  3. Drag it into place, tap Place, then save.

Why use it here

  • Free, with no sign-up and no watermark.
  • Your files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.
  • Install it as an app and use it offline after the first visit.

Questions

Is this a legally binding digital signature?It places an image of your handwritten signature. Whether that is accepted depends on who asks for it.
Can I sign several pages?Yes. Place a signature on each page you need.
Is my document uploaded?No. Signing happens in your browser.
